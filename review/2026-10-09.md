### Verdict

The engineering is solid and the Q/H separation discipline is better than most
published products. The **indicator math has four real bugs** (all reproduced
below), the **stated purpose of the project is not served by anything on the
page** (there is no time series and no trend anywhere), and **the hydrological
year is wrong for Poland** — which is not a detail but a systemic error touching
every year-indexed number and chart on the site.

### 1. Domain correctness

#### 1.1 What is right

- Two-stage characteristic-flow matrix is the correct *family* — IMGW-PIB calls
  first-order values (per hydrological year) **I rzędu** and their multi-year
  aggregation **II rzędu**; the project's implementation matches that
  construction, including the ≥300-valid-day year filter.
- `hydro_year` labelling semantics (Oct→year+1) is internally consistent; the
  `cal` window really does mean hydrological years 1991–2020, not calendar.
- Q/H strict separation is honoured end-to-end (template, gating, chart set).
  No cross-variable fallback found.
- Eckhardt (2005) recursion is written correctly (α=0.925, BF_max=0.8,
  clamp to `[0, y_t]`), BFI = ΣBF/ΣQ as documented.
- Colwell's identity is implemented correctly in exact arithmetic
  (P = C + M follows from H(X|Y) = H(XY) − H(Y)).
- Flashiness numerator is correctly restricted to consecutive-day pairs.
- Pardé coefficient is the right ratio (monthly mean ÷ mean of the record).
- FDC exceedance axis and ordering are correct.
- Spot-check vs reality: Sandomierz annual mean (SSQ) = 287 m³/s, yearly min of
  minima 57, max of maxima 5 040 — all plausible for Wisła at km 655.
- Raster hydrograph and timing chart use hydrological-year axes; hydrogram y-axis
  is no longer scientific notation.

#### 1.2 Bug: rolling means are computed on a gap-stripped sub-series

`aggregates.py:_q_blocks` and `_stage_block` do `qq = q[q_valid]` and then
`qq.rolling(n, min_periods=n)`. Because the clean series is reindexed to a
continuous daily grid with `NaN` in gaps, dropping the `NaN` rows means the
window no longer spans `n` **days** — it spans `n` **observations**, which can be
weeks or months of calendar time.

Reproduced on real data:

| station | window | current (sub-series) | correct (daily grid) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 150210170 Sandomierz (225 gaps) | 90-d max | **1110.19** | **1092.03** |
| 149180050 (188 gaps, longest 367 d) | 90-d min | **0.1** | **0.2** |

The second case is off by a factor of 2. This directly corrupts
`annual_extremes` (`min7/max7/min30/max30/min90/max90`) and every H
`extremes` row on the page — i.e. the tables headed „Ekstrema roczne" are not
extremes of 7/30/90-day means.

Fix: compute `q.rolling(n, min_periods=n)` on the **daily-reindexed** series and
`dropna()` — windows containing a gap then disappear on their own.

#### 1.3 Bug: day-of-year climatology misaligns after February in leap years

`aggregates.py:323` groups by `ddates.dt.dayofyear` and then drops index 60
("Feb 29"). In a leap year 1 March has `dayofyear == 61`, in a common year it is
60. Measured composition of the groups for Sandomierz:

- group 60 = `03-01` ×56 + `02-29` ×19  → the code deletes **March 1**
- group 61 = `03-02` ×56 + `03-01` ×19  → March 1 and March 2 are blended
- …and every later day of the year is similarly shifted/blended

Consequences: `dynamics.min_daily` / `max_daily` (the „Min/Max dz" row) are
wrong — impl: **181.4 / 510.9**, correct month-day grouping: **179.4 / 505.3**.
Same defect exists in `charts.py` only via `_hy_dayofyear`, which is fine
(it counts from a fixed 1 Oct), so the charts and the table disagree with each
other for March–December.

Fix: group by `(month, day)` or by hydrological day-of-year from a fixed epoch.

#### 1.4 Bug: Colwell's Constancy is pinned by construction, not measured

`_contingency` bins flow into 4 states using the record's own 10th/90th
percentiles. That forces the marginal distribution of states to ~(0.10, 0.80,
0.10) for every perennial station, so `H(X)` — and therefore
`C = 1 − H(X)/ln 4` — is a constant.

Measured across all 1032 `q_max` variants with a Colwell block:

```
C = 0.54 → 730 stations
C = 0.55 → 174
C = 0.56 →  48
C = 0.53 →  33
(…everything else: 47 stations combined)
```

88 % of stations land in [0.54, 0.56]. **Constancy carries no hydrological
signal on this site.** The module docstring also contradicts itself: it says
equal-frequency binning „was rejected because it forces C→0", then implements
exactly equal-frequency binning via q10/q90.

Recommendation: bin against *hydrologically* meaningful thresholds (zero flow,
e.g. Q95-derived low-flow class, mean, Q10 high-flow) with fixed reference
period, and say on the page which binning was used — otherwise Colwell P/C/M
is not comparable between stations and should be dropped.

#### 1.5 Bug: P ≠ C + M as displayed

Values are rounded independently. In **208 of 1032** `q_max` variants the
printed triple violates the identity the docstring claims is exact, e.g.
`0.57 / 0.53 / 0.03` (sums to 0.56), `0.64 / 0.55 / 0.10` (sums to 0.65).
Fix: round C and M, then derive P = C + M from the rounded pair (or display
an explicit ±0.01 note).

#### 1.6 Gap-independence of the recursive/temporal statistics

`eckhardt_baseflow`, `qq.autocorr(lag=1)` and `_pulse_stats`' run detection all
operate on the gap-stripped sub-series. For the two stations checked the numeric
effect was small (lag-1: 0.6037 vs 0.6040), and `_pulse_stats` correctly
re-segments on `dates.diff() == 1`. Still: the Eckhardt recursion treats a
367-day gap as one time step, so for sparse stations BFI is not defined
correctly. Either split into contiguous segments and seed each, or document the
approximation explicitly (the docstring documents the flashiness approximation
but not this one).

#### 1.7 The `cal` gate counts years with *any* data, not usable years

`variant_frames` admits `*_cal` when `why[q_valid].nunique() >= 20`. Measured on
one cal window: 30 hydrological years had some Q, but the thinnest of them had
only **92 valid days**. A cal variant can therefore be labelled
„1991–2020" while resting on ~90-day years. Count years with ≥300 valid days
(the same threshold the flow matrix already uses).

#### 1.8 Statistically thin flow matrix

`flow_matrix` returns values from as few as **5** hydrological years with
≥300 days. „Charakterystyki charakterystyczne" from five years are not
comparable between stations and will not match anything published. Prawo wodne
art. 270 ust. 6 defines *wielolecie* as **at least 20 hydrological years**; the
IMGW-PIB monograph recommends a homogeneous **30-year** period for SNQ.
Recommendation: raise the floor (≥20) and print the actual period under the
matrix.

#### 1.9 Confusing / undefined indicator labels on the page

Rendered (Sandomierz, `q_max`):

```
Baseflow Index (BFI) ....... 0,78
...
Baseflow ................... 0,22
Min/Max dz [m³/s] .......... 181 / 511
```

- Two baseflow numbers with near-identical names that look contradictory. They
  are different metrics: ΣBF/ΣQ vs *share of days with BF/Q ≥ 0.9*. Neither has
  a definition on the page. Rename (`Wskaźnik przepływu podstawowego` vs
  `dni z przepływem podstawowym ≥ 90 %`) and add a tooltip/glossary.
- „Min/Max dz" is not an IHA parameter and is not defined anywhere (README issue
  6 already flags the intent as unconfirmed). It also sits inside a section
  headed **„Wybrane wskaźniki ekohydrologiczne (IHA)"**, which it is not part
  of. Same for `Baseflow`.
- `Zero-flow` has no unit (dni) and is untranslated; `High/Low pul`,
  `Rise/Fall`, `Reversals`, `Zero-flow` are English inside an otherwise Polish
  reference. `High/Low pul` in particular reads as a truncated word.
- Rise/Fall prints a signed fall (`−35,3`). State the sign convention or print
  magnitudes.
- Matrix cells are unlabelled: a reader cannot see that the cell at (S, W) is
  **SWQ**. Given the two-stage definition is not self-evident, print the
  abbreviation in each cell or in a caption.
- Half of the indicator vocabulary is unexplained (Colwell, Flashiness,
  Reversals, Pardé, BFI). For an *encyclopedic* source this is the main
  usability gap — a one-line definition per row is enough.

#### 1.10 Chart-level domain notes

- `chart_boxplots` sets `showfliers=False`. Flood peaks and recession floors are
  exactly the signal in a boxplot; hiding them removes most of the information.
- Spectrogram x-axis is "dni od początku serii", not a year axis — inconsistent
  with everything else and unusable for locating a specific flood year.
- Monthly means **table** is in calendar order (Sty→Gru) while Pardé, boxplots
  and raster are in hydrological order (X→IX). Same page, two orderings.
- `chart_raster` gives the y-axis the label "rok hydrologiczny"; timing chart
  runs years upward, raster runs 1951 at top → the direction clash already
  noted under UI is a data-presentation inconsistency, not just cosmetics.

### 2. Value — is the right information shown, and what is missing?

#### 2.1 High-value things that are present (keep)

- Coverage-adaptive Q/H pages: genuinely the right call, and better than most
  official products, which silently mix variables.
- Per-variant period menu (`maks. seria` vs `1991–2020`).
- Station index + separate lake index (user-ratified on 10-05) — executed.
- `Dane Q` / `Dane H` availability rows always both present, with days and
  coverage %.
- Kilometrage, coordinates, Leaflet marker, status.
- Flow duration curve, raster hydrograph, Pardé, timing of extremes, spectrogram
  — all of these are the right chart set for a regime atlas.
- Polish number formatting (grouped thousands, decimal comma, `−` U+2212) is
  correct and consistent.

#### 2.2 Critical omission: there is no time series and no trend anywhere

The project's stated purpose (README *Intent*) is that long-term trends are
"scattered and hard to find" and that this site will be the reference source for
them. **Nothing on the site shows a trend.**

- The chart set is `raster, fdc, parde, spectrogram, timing, boxplots`. There is
  **no plot of annual mean discharge through time**, no annual minimum / annual
  maximum series, no table of per-year values.
- The annual mean exists only as a *single number* (SSQ in the matrix). A single
  scalar cannot show a trend.
- No trend test (Mann–Kendall, Spearman, Theil–Sen) anywhere; `mocks/info.md`
  lists SSFI and „typologia lat hydrologicznych" as future candidates but even
  the plain annual series is missing.
- The raster hydrograph is the closest thing — and it is a colour image, not
  numbers a reader can cite.

This is the single highest-value addition available: a „Przepływy roczne"
chart + table (year, Qśr, Qmin, Qmax, n dni) per variant, plus a trend line.
Without it the atlas cannot deliver its own premise.

#### 2.3 Critical omission: no low-flow / drought statistics

Polish water management is organised around low flow:

- **SNQ** (średni niski przepływ z wielolecia) is legally load-bearing —
  Prawo wodne art. 270 ust. 6 ties water-service charges to it and defines
  *wielolecie* as ≥20 hydrological years; art. 403 ust. 7 sets
  **przepływ nienaruszalny = 50 % SNQ**.
- Hydrological drought is legally *flow below SNQ* (rozporządzenie MI
  24.10.2023, Dz.U. poz. 2430).
- The monograph's taxonomy of Polish flow characteristics is:
  charakterystyczne główne / o określonym czasie trwania / o określonym
  prawdopodobieństwie przekroczenia i nieosiągnięcia / gwarantowane.

The site shows **none** of these. No SNQ, no Q90/Q95, no low-flow duration, no
exceedance-probability flows. A Polish hydrologist looking for Q95 on this site
finds nothing, while the FDC chart actually contains the information — it just
isn't read out as a number.

Note on notation: state the convention explicitly. Polish sources use
*prawdopodobieństwo przekroczenia* and *nieosiągnięcia* and Q90 is
read differently in each; ambiguity here silently flips low flow into high flow.

#### 2.4 Critical omission: water temperature is parsed and then thrown away

`ingest/archive.py` maps column 9 to `temp_c`, but `clean_station` keeps only
`date, stage_cm, discharge_m3s`. Measured over 60 monthly archives
(1 414 191 rows): **26.2 % (369 915 rows) carry a real temperature value**.

Rocznik Hydrologiczny publishes a complete temperature table per station
(WT/ST/NT, daily + monthly/half-year/annual characteristics, 0.1 °C). This is a
whole Rocznik section the atlas silently discards, and it is directly relevant
to the ice/low-flow story.

#### 2.5 Critical omission: no datum, so stage numbers are not usable

Rocznik publishes **P.z.** — *rzędna zera wodowskazu* — for every station,
referenced to **NAP (Normaal Amsterdams Peil)**, to 0.01 m. The site shows
`Stan minimalny 42 cm` with no reference level at all.

This is not cosmetic: **14 stations have negative stage**, the most negative
being **−38 cm** (station 152229997). Without P.z. and the reference system a
negative stage is indistinguishable from a data error.

Same class of problem: `Stan minimalny 42 cm` and `1-d min/max 42 / 820 cm` are
two rows saying the same thing — the redundant row is where P.z. should be.

#### 2.6 Critical omission: no `uwagi` (measurement notes / anthropopressure)

Rocznik has a **Wykaz uwag** covering "sposób prowadzenia pomiarów i obserwacji,
a także o antropopresji i innych czynnikach mających wpływ na wartości pomiarów".
The atlas has nothing equivalent.

This is the same request the user already made on **2026-10-05**:
"for stations which changed characteristics because of hydroengineering there
should be a separation of before/after change series. Otherwise IHA make no
sense." **Not implemented.** `q_max` still pools the whole record across
regulation, reservoir commissioning, gauge relocation and rating-curve changes
— so IHA pulse/reversal/rate numbers for regulated stations are not meaningful.
Rocznik also warns explicitly that IMGW retro-updates archived Q when the
stage–discharge relation changes.

Minimal version: a per-station `uwagi:` list in `stations-meta.yaml`, rendered
as a visible block, plus an optional `breaks:` list that splits `max` into
before/after variants.

#### 2.7 Missing station metadata (already known — but it is more blocking than it looks)

Every curated field is `null`: `catchment_km2`, `regime`, `station_class`,
`unit_runoff_dm3s_km2`, `runoff_mm`, `network_density_km_km2`,
`river_length_km`, `annual_precip_mm`. Consequences beyond the hidden rows:

- No `A` ⇒ no Q/P, no specific discharge ⇒ **stations cannot be compared with
  each other at all**. Raw m³/s across basins of 40 km² and 84 000 km² is
  not an atlas, it is a data dump.
- No `regime` ⇒ a snow-fed and a rain-fed river look identical.
- Rocznik's wykaz stacji publishes **A, P.z., Źródło pomiaru (L = limnimetr /
  O = obserwator), km** as first-class columns. The atlas has km only.
- `station_class` renders as „{{.}} rzędu" — I could **not** verify a
  I/II/III station-class convention in Prawo wodne, the Rocznik or the
  monograph. Confirm the intended source before spending effort filling it;
  „rzędu" may be a mis-transcription of the *charakterystyki I rzędu*
  vocabulary. (Field is currently always null, so nothing is displayed.)

#### 2.8 Missing duration flows (Rocznik's standard set)

Rocznik publishes *przepływ o określonym czasie trwania* for
**10, 30, 60, 90, 120, 180, 240, 270, 300, 330, 355, 364 days**.
The atlas computes min/max of **1/ 3/ 7/ 30/ 90**-day rolling means. Different
purpose (extremes of a window vs. a duration series), and the long durations
(120–364 d) — the ones that matter for drought and reservoir sizing — are absent
entirely.

#### 2.9 Missing half-year (`półrocze`) characteristic values

Rocznik computes characteristic values for **miesiąc, półrocze i rok**, where
the half-years are **XI–IV (zimowe)** and **V–X (letnie)**. The atlas has
calendar-month means (correct, matches Rocznik) and hydrological-year aggregates,
but no half-year values. Half-years are what make the Polish regime
characterisation recognisable.

#### 2.10 Navigation / index value gaps

- **No link from a station page to its river page.** `Rzeka` is plain text; the
  only links on the page are `/`, `/indeks/`, `/jeziora/` and the variant
  query. The whole river hierarchy is one-way.
- **`voivodeship` is in `stations.json` and never rendered** — a free, already
  computed piece of standard administrative context for a Polish atlas.
- **`/indeks/` has no search, no sort, no filter** — 1372 rows of static HTML
  with a single `version-menu.js` on the site. Finding one station means
  Ctrl-F.
- Header `Q (lata)` shows `1951–2025`, which is a *range*, not a count of years.
  `Q (okres)` or a second column with the count would be unambiguous.
- River hub list disambiguates duplicate names as bare `Biała 5 / Biała 2 /
  Biała 1`; each should carry its basin or mouth („Biała (dopływ Wisły)").
- River pages show no mouth, no length, no catchment area — the three things a
  reader expects from an atlas entry.
- First index row has `Rzeka = "-"` (Szabelnia).

#### 2.11 Non-blocking

- `hugo.toml` has no `baseURL` ⇒ `sitemap.xml` emits `<loc>//localhost:1313/…</loc>`
  (no scheme, localhost host). Will silently produce an unusable sitemap on
  publish.
- `period` is computed and written into every aggregates JSON but never read by
  any template — either use it (print it under each block) or drop it.
- `_q_digits` rounding is applied to stage as well as discharge, so
  `142.5 cm → 143`.

### 3. Alignment with Polish standards

#### 3.1 BLOCKER — the hydrological year is wrong

The project uses **1 Oct – 30 Sep** everywhere (`aggregates.hydro_year`,
`charts.HY_MONTH_STARTS/HY_MONTH_LABELS`, `_hy_dayofyear`, the `cal` window,
README).

Poland uses **1 November – 31 October**:

- Rocznik Hydrologiczny 2025, „Objaśnienia skrótów i symboli":
  `Rok – Okres XI-X`, `Zima – Okres XI-IV`, `Lato – Okres V-X`.
- PGW Wody Polskie (with an IMGW synoptyk-hydrolog): „w hydrologii… rok nie
  zaczyna się zimą, lecz późną jesienią, **dokładnie 1 listopada**", with the
  physical rationale (retencja śniegowa, koniec wegetacji „na przełomie
  października i listopada").
- Not a statutory definition — "rok hydrologiczny" does not appear in Prawo
  wodne 2017 — but it is unambiguous IMGW/Wody Polskie practice, and this site
  claims IMGW as its source.

Scope of the error (all affected):

| location | current | should be |
|---|---|---|
| `aggregates.hydro_year` | Oct 1 → y+1 | Nov 1 → y+1 |
| flow matrix year grouping | Oct–Sep years | Nov–Oct years |
| pulses / reversals per-year counts | Oct–Sep | Nov–Oct |
| `variant_frames` cal window | HY 1991–2020 = Oct 1990–Sep 2020 | HY 1991–2020 = Nov 1990–Oct 2020 |
| `charts.HY_MONTH_STARTS/LABELS` | `X, XI, XII, I … IX` | `XI, XII, I … X` |
| `charts._hy_dayofyear` | day 1 = 1 Oct | day 1 = 1 Nov |
| `chart_parde`, `chart_boxplots`, `chart_raster`, `chart_timing` month order | starts at X | starts at XI |
| README „Hydrological year" decision row | Oct 1 → Sep 30 | Nov 1 → Oct 31 |

Because this is used for year labels, second-order aggregation and every chart
axis, **every year-indexed number on the site changes** once it is fixed.
Do it before any further indicator work, and re-run `hydroatlas build`.

(Worth noting: 1 Oct is the US water year. The convention appears to have been
imported rather than derived from Polish practice.)

#### 3.2 Characteristic flows: right framework, incomplete set

Verified in IMGW-PIB monograph §B.2.1:

> „Przepływy główne I rzędu dla przepływów dobowych określa się następująco:
> **NQ** – przepływ niski (minimalny); **SQ** – przepływ średni;
> **ZQ** – przepływ zwyczajny (mediana…); **WQ** – przepływ wielki (maksymalny)."

and §B.2.2:

> „Obliczając **cztery** charakterystyki główne dla czterech ciągów przepływów
> głównych I rzędu: NQ, SQ, ZQ i WQ, uzyskuje się **16 wartości** przepływów
> głównych II rzędu."

So the Polish canonical matrix is **4 × 4 = 16**, not 3 × 3 = 9. The project
omits **ZQ (przepływ zwyczajny / mediana)** at both orders.

Two mitigating facts: the monograph also notes ZQ is „określany rzadko ze
w względu na małe wykorzystanie w praktyce inżynierskiej", and the Rocznik's
published abbreviation table lists `WQ/SQ/NQ` (and for stages `WWW/SWW/NNW`,
`WW/SW/NW`, `SSW`, `SNW`) without ZQ. So a 3-wide matrix is defensible — but
the page must **state which definition it uses**, because a reader who knows
the monograph will count cells and get 9 where the standard says 16.

Also from the monograph, correctly followed: I-rzędu is computed for
„zwykle… roku hydrologicznego", and the basic period must "rozpocząć się w tym
samym dniu i miesiącu roku hydrologicznego" for all values — satisfied once
3.1 is fixed.

#### 3.3 Precision: not to Rocznik standard

Rocznik: „Wartości przepływu są określone **z dokładnością do trzech cyfr
znaczących**, przy wartościach mniejszych od 0,05 m³/s z dokładnością do
0,001 m³/s." and „Wartości stanów wody podawane są z dokładnością do **1 cm**."

`_q_digits` rounds to an **integer whenever |v| ≥ 10**:

- `15.8 → 16` — 2 significant digits; Rocznik prints `15.8`
- `0.158 → 0.16` — 2 significant digits; Rocznik prints `0.158`
- `0.048 → 0.05` — should keep 3 decimals below 0.05 m³/s, giving `0.048`
- `158 → 158` ✓, `2040 → 2040` ✓

Fix: round to 3 significant digits, with the 0.05 m³/s / 0.001 m³/s exception.
Stage: keep 1 cm (currently satisfied by accident of the same rounding).

#### 3.4 Missing P.z. / reference system (see 2.5)

Rocznik wykaz stacji column 8: „Rzędną P.z. wodowskazu odniesioną do średniego
poziomu Morza Północnego… (Normaal Amsterdams Peil), Holandia (z dokładnością do
0,01 m)". Not present. Add a `pz_m_nap` field (and note the reference system
once in a site-wide legend rather than repeating it per row).

| Rocznik content | atlas |
|---|---|
| Wykaz stacji: kod, rzeka, stacja, źródło pomiaru (L/O), km, **A**, **P.z.** | km ✓, coords ✓ (extra), **A ✗, P.z. ✗, źródło ✗** |
| Wykaz uwag (pomiar, antropopresja) | ✗ |
| Charakterystyka roku hydrologicznego | ✗ |
| Daily + characteristic **stage** + **ice phenomena** | Q/H aggregates ✓, **ice ✗** |
| Daily + characteristic **discharge** + duration flows 10…364 d | aggregates ✓, **duration ✗** |
| Daily + characteristic **water temperature** (WT/ST/NT) | **✗ (data discarded)** |
| Characteristic values for **miesiąc / półrocze / rok** | miesiąc ✓, **półrocze ✗**, rok ✓ |
| Extremes with **dates of occurrence**, `zator` / `WW` tags | ✗ (timing chart is close) |
| Values missing marked `−` | "—" ✓ (good) |

Ice phenomena are genuinely **not present in the `codz_*` source files** (the
columns are id, name, river, y, m, d, stage, discharge, temp, code — no ice
field), so this is a known source limitation rather than an implementation
error; it should be recorded as such in README, and ice also matters because
the monograph requires reduction coefficients: „W okresach występowania zjawisk
lodowych lub zarastania koryt roślinnością przepływ obliczany jest z
zastosowaniem współczynników redukcyjnych."

#### 3.6 Legal/statistical terms absent

- **SNQ** and *wielolecie* ≥ 20 hydrological years (art. 270 ust. 6) — verified
  in the statute text.

#### 3.7 Terminology and wording

Correct and matching Rocznik:

- `Status: czynna` — Rocznik says „stacje hydrologiczne czynne" ✓.
  `archiwalna` is not Rocznik wording (it uses „archiwizowane dane") but is
  clear and acceptable.
- decimal comma, `m³/s`, `cm`, **doba** (`Rise/Fall [m³/s/dobę]`) ✓
  — „doba" is the correct Polish 24 h unit.
- month abbreviations Sty/Lut/… ✓, km, km², dm³/s·km² ✓.

- Mixed PL/EN label set inside one table (see 1.9).
- `Wskaźnik odpływu (H)` — the `(H)` qualifier is unexplained and reads as a
  variable tag; say what it means or drop it.
- `Typ stacji … rzędu` — see 2.7, source unverified.

#### 3.8 Indicators: which are real and correctly used

Verified as genuine, established methods (implementations checked):

- **Colwell P/C/M** — Colwell 1974, *Ecology*; formulas in the docstring are
  right. The *binning* is the problem (1.4).
- **BFI / Eckhardt filter** — Eckhardt 2008, *J. Hydrol.*; recursion correct.
  α = 0.925 with BF_max = 0.8 is a defensible parameter set; state it on the
  page (parameters change BFI materially).
- **Flashiness (Richards–Baker)** — Baker et al. 2004, *JAWRA* ✓.
- **Pardé coefficients** — used in Polish literature (e.g. Vistula studies,
  *J. Hydrology Hydromechanics* 2017); definition monthly mean ÷ annual mean
  matches usage here.
- **IHA** — Richter et al. 1996 described **32** parameters in five groups;
  the commonly cited **33** is the software figure. The monthly-means group
  legitimately *is* IHA Group 1, and pulses / rise-fall / reversals are IHA
  Groups 4–5, so the heading is broadly right — but `Min/Max dz` and `Baseflow`
  are not IHA and should be moved out of that section.
